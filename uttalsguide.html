10 äma lirë AR kamïn △ /ˈæːma ˈliːrə ar kaˈmiːn/ Loving heart around the fireplace Emotion + preposition + det

System-coverage

  • Mening 1, 2: Possessiv (MAL) + storlekspar (MIN/MAX)
  • Mening 3, 4: Compound nouns (Rule 7) — solya+arba=solros, mane+solya=solförmörkelse
  • Mening 5: MUN-modifier + preposition (kätna) + 2 determinativ (○, △)
  • Mening 6: Räkneord (VA, TA) + preposition (ME)
  • Mening 7, 8: Emotion-glyphs (lela, vira) framför logogram
  • Mening 9, 10: Emotion (kira, äma) + preposition + determinativ
